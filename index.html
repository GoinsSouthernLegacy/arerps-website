About A.R.E.R.P.S.

A.R.E.R.P.S. is a high-realism roleplay experience built around real Tennessee locations, with emergency services at its heart. AI-driven NPCs, SPRAID robotic officers and medics, holographic interfaces and dynamic weather and day/night cycles make every shift different.

🤖 AI-Driven NPCs

Context-aware police, EMS, security and civilian robots that react to what is happening around them.

🔮 Holographic Tech

Holo phones, dispatch terminals, street signs, billboards and vehicle HUDs.

🌤️ Living World

Dynamic weather and day/night, realistic roads, bridges, buildings and interiors.

📱 Cross-Platform

Designed for PC, mobile, controller and VR players.

    Community Rules

    1. Respect every member. No harassment, hate speech or discrimination.
    2. Stay in character during active scenes (no fail-roleplay, no metagaming).
    3. Follow department chain of command and SOPs.
    4. No exploiting, cheating or abusing bugs. Report them instead.
    5. Value life: do not randomly kill (no RDM / VDM).
    6. Keep content age-appropriate and follow platform Terms of Service.
    7. Staff decisions can be appealed through the appeal process.
